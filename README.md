# Business Operations Performance Analysis

## Business Analyst Portfolio Project

### Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Analyst workflow using a simulated business operations dataset containing 1,500 transactions.

The objective was to evaluate operational performance, identify trends and potential inefficiencies, develop key performance indicators (KPIs), and translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

---

## Business Problem

Business leaders need a centralized view of operational performance to understand revenue, order activity, processing efficiency, cancellations, and delivery performance.

Without consistent reporting and analysis, it can be difficult to identify operational bottlenecks and determine where process improvements may have the greatest impact.

---

## Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to:

- Measure overall operational performance
- Develop meaningful business KPIs
- Analyze revenue by product category and region
- Identify cancellation patterns
- Evaluate processing efficiency
- Measure on-time delivery performance
- Identify trends and potential improvement opportunities
- Translate data findings into business recommendations

# Key Findings

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | $4,244,781.74 |
| Total Orders | 1,500 |
| Average Order Value | $2,829.85 |
| Cancellation Rate | 16.00% |
| Average Processing Time | 5.54 days |
| On-Time Delivery Rate | 91.90% |

### Revenue Performance

Kitchen was the largest revenue-producing product category, generating approximately $1.73 million in revenue.

The South region generated the highest total revenue among the four regions.

### Cancellation Performance

The overall cancellation rate was 16%.

Customer-requested cancellations represented the largest cancellation category, followed by pricing-related cancellations.

### Operational Performance

Average processing time was 5.54 days.

The analysis showed differences in processing time across product categories, creating an opportunity to investigate workflow bottlenecks.

The on-time delivery rate was 91.90%, indicating that most non-cancelled orders were delivered on time while leaving an opportunity to investigate delayed orders.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the organization could:

1. Investigate the primary drivers of customer-requested cancellations.
2. Review pricing-related cancellations for potential pricing or quoting process improvements.
3. Analyze product categories with longer processing times to identify workflow bottlenecks.
4. Investigate delayed orders to identify recurring operational issues.
5. Establish centralized KPI reporting for ongoing performance monitoring.
6. Perform additional segmentation by region, customer segment, product category, and sales representative.
