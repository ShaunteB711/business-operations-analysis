# Business Requirements Document
## Business Operations Performance Analysis

### 1. Business Problem

The organization currently lacks a centralized view of operational performance. Business leaders need a clearer understanding of sales activity, order volume, revenue, processing times, cancellations, and performance trends.

Without consistent reporting, it can be difficult to identify operational bottlenecks, performance issues, and opportunities for improvement.

### 2. Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze business operations data and create a reporting solution that helps stakeholders understand operational performance and make data-driven decisions.

### 3. Stakeholders

- Operations Managers
- Business Managers
- Sales Leaders
- Process Improvement Teams
- Business Analysts
- Senior Leadership

### 4. Key Business Questions

1. How much revenue is being generated?
2. How many orders are being processed?
3. What is the average order value?
4. What percentage of orders are cancelled?
5. How long does it take to process orders?
6. What percentage of orders are processed on time?
7. Are there noticeable performance trends?
8. Where are the largest operational opportunities?

### 5. Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Cancellation Rate
- Average Processing Time
- On-Time Processing Rate

### 6. Functional Requirements

The analysis should:

- Clean and prepare the dataset.
- Calculate operational KPIs.
- Identify trends and patterns.
- Analyze performance across relevant business categories.
- Identify potential operational bottlenecks.
- Provide data-driven recommendations.

### 7. Reporting Requirements

The final solution should provide:

- KPI summary
- Revenue analysis
- Order volume analysis
- Processing performance
- Cancellation analysis
- Trend analysis
- Business recommendations

### 8. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if stakeholders can use the analysis to:

- Quickly understand operational performance.
- Identify areas requiring improvement.
- Monitor key business KPIs.
- Understand performance trends.
- Make informed operational decisions.

### 9. Assumptions

- The dataset represents simulated business operations.
- Data is assumed to be sufficiently representative for analytical purposes.
- No personally identifiable information is included.
- Findings are intended for portfolio demonstration purposes.

### 10. Expected Deliverables

- Business Requirements Document
- Data analysis
- SQL queries
- Python analysis
- Excel analysis
- Tableau dashboard
- Business insights
- Recommendations
